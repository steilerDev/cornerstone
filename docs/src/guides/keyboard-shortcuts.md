# Keyboard Shortcuts

Cornerstone has keyboard shortcuts for moving around and acting quickly. The shortcuts that work on the page you are viewing are listed in the **Keyboard shortcuts** overlay.

## Open the Overlay

Use any of these ways to open the overlay:

- Press `?` on any page.
- On desktop, open your avatar menu and choose **Keyboard shortcuts**.
- On a phone or tablet with a hardware keyboard attached, open **More** and choose **Keyboard shortcuts**. This row only appears when a keyboard is attached.

Press `Esc` to close the overlay.

## Read the Overlay

The overlay has up to three groups:

| Group | What it lists |
|-------|---------------|
| **On this page** | Shortcuts for the page you are viewing, such as `n` on a list page or `e` on a detail page. Shows "This page has no shortcuts of its own." when the page has none. |
| **Everywhere** | Shortcuts that work on every page. |
| **Go to** | Shortcuts that open a main area of the app. |

## Everywhere

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `Ctrl K` (Windows, Linux) or `⌘K` (Mac) | Search everywhere |
| `/` | Focus search. On a list page, the search covers that list. |
| `n` | Open the New menu to create a task, purchase, milestone and more. On a list page, `n` creates an item of that list instead. |
| `?` | Show keyboard shortcuts |
| `Esc` | Close the open dialog, menu or search |
| `Ctrl Z` (Windows, Linux) or `⌘Z` (Mac) | Undo the last change |

## Go to

Press `g`, then one of the letters below, within about 1.5 seconds. Pressing any other key, or waiting longer, cancels the sequence without opening anything.

| Then press | Opens |
|------------|-------|
| `h` | Home |
| `t` | Tasks |
| `s` | Schedule (the Gantt chart) |
| `p` | Purchases |
| `d` | Site diary |
| `f` | Photos |
| `c` | Companies |
| `m` | Money |

Only the destinations your account can open are listed.

## On a Page

Each page can add its own shortcuts, which appear under **On this page**. The most common ones are:

| Shortcut | Where | Action |
|----------|-------|--------|
| `e` | Task, purchase and milestone pages | Edit the details |
| `Delete` or `Backspace` | Task, purchase and milestone pages | Delete the item, after you confirm |
| `n` | List pages | Create a new item in that list |

On list pages, `Delete` and `Backspace` do nothing. To delete a record from a list, open its **⋯** menu and choose **Delete**. That dialog always asks first.

See the guide for each area for the details, for example [Tasks](work-items/).

## Turn Off Single-Key Shortcuts

Shortcuts that use a single letter or symbol are easy to press by accident while you are reading or scrolling. To turn them off, open the overlay and switch off **Single-key shortcuts** in its footer.

With the switch off:

- `/`, `n`, `?`, `e` and `g` followed by a letter do nothing.
- Each of those rows is marked **Turned off** in the overlay.
- `Ctrl K` or `⌘K`, `Ctrl Z` or `⌘Z`, `Esc`, `Delete` and `Backspace`, and the **Keyboard shortcuts** row in the avatar menu or More sheet keep working.

Turn the switch back on to restore the single-key shortcuts. The setting is saved in the browser you use, so other browsers and devices keep their own setting.

## When Shortcuts Do Not Fire

Shortcuts never run while you type in a text field, such as a title, note or search box. While a dialog is open, the shortcuts of the page behind it wait. Press `Esc` to close the dialog.

:::tip
Press `?` on any page to see the shortcuts available there.
:::
