# Construction Diary

The construction diary (Bautagebuch) is where you keep a chronological record of your building project in your own words. The **Site diary** lists the entries you write. Changes that Cornerstone records by itself, such as a task being finished or an invoice being paid, are listed on the [History](automatic-events) page.

## Overview

The diary provides:

- **Manual Entries** -- Write daily logs, record site visits, track deliveries, flag issues, and add general notes
- **Week-by-Week List** -- The Site diary groups your entries by week, newest first, with photos shown first in each row
- **History Link** -- A link at the top of the Site diary opens the History page, where automatic changes are listed
- **Photo Attachments** -- Capture photos on your phone and tag them with a caption, area, and compass orientation for visual documentation
- **Photo Annotation** -- Mark up photos with rectangles, arrows, text, measurements, and more, directly in the browser (touch-enabled)
- **Unfinished Drafts** -- Entries you started but did not finish are listed in an **Unfinished** row at the top of the Site diary, where you can continue or discard them
- **Signature Capture** -- Collect digital signatures from users or vendors with a drawing canvas; signed entries become immutable
- **Infinite Scroll** -- Entries load automatically as you scroll; a "Load more" button lets you fetch the next batch without scrolling
- **Filters** -- Narrow the Site diary by entry type, area, company, and date range

## Entry Types

### Manual Entries

You create these entries to document activities and observations:

| Type | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Daily Log** | General daily progress notes and observations |
| **Site Visit** | Record a visit to the construction site |
| **Delivery** | Track materials or items delivered to the site |
| **Issue** | Flag a problem that needs attention -- supports acknowledgment signatures |
| **General Note** | Anything that does not fit the other categories |

## The Site Diary List

Open **Site diary** from the navigation to see your entries. The list has three parts: the Unfinished row, the search and filters, and the entries grouped by week.

### Weeks

Entries are grouped by week, Monday to Sunday, based on the entry date. The newest week is at the top:

- The most recent week is headed **This week**, the week before it **Last week**
- Older weeks are headed **Week of** and the Monday of that week, for example *Week of March 2*
- Under each heading, a muted line gives the full range of days, for example *March 2 – 8, 2026*

A week keeps one heading even when its entries continue onto the next page of the list.

### Entry Rows

Each entry is one row:

- The first thing you see is the photos: up to five thumbnails, followed by a **+n** tile when the entry has more
- Next to the photos are the coloured type label (Daily Log, Site Visit, Delivery, Issue, or General Note), the entry date, and the title
- A short line of text, the facts the entry records (such as the company or the number of workers), and the areas its photos are tagged with
- Signed entries are marked as signed

Select a row to open its [entry page](#the-entry-page). An entry without a title shows its type and date instead, for example *Site Visit · March 9*.

### Unfinished Row

When you have drafts, an **Unfinished** box appears above the week list. A draft is an entry you started but did not finish. Drafts are kept out of the week list so your diary stays focused on finished entries.

- **Continue** opens the draft in the capture screen so you can finish it
- **Discard** deletes the draft. A confirmation asks you to **Keep entry** or **Discard**, and tells you how many photos are deleted with it. Photos still waiting to upload are deleted too
- **Show all** (with the number of drafts) lists only your drafts in the feed. Each draft row shows a **Draft** label and opens the capture screen. To go back to the normal list, remove the **Unfinished** filter chip or select **Clear all**

The Unfinished row is hidden when you have no drafts, and while the feed shows only drafts.

### Searching and Filtering

Use the search field to find entries by their text. Select **Filters** to narrow the list further:

- **Type** -- Show only some of the manual entry types, such as Site Visit or Delivery. Only the types you have used are offered
- **Area** -- Show entries with at least one photo tagged with that area or with an area inside it
- **Company** -- Show the entries that name a company, such as a daily log for that company or a delivery from it
- **Dates** -- Set **From** and **To** to show only entries in that range. If **From** is after **To**, the list keeps the last valid range and shows a message under the dates

On a phone, the filters open in a sheet. On a larger screen they open in a panel next to the list. Filters apply as soon as you change them. Below the search field, each active filter appears as a chip you can remove with its **×** button, and **Clear all** removes every filter and the search. If no entries match, the list says so and offers **Clear filters**.

## The Entry Page

Select an entry to open its page. The page is ordered from the most visible content to the details:

1. **Photos** -- the photos come first, as a grid. If there are none, the page says *No photos attached yet.* and offers **Add photos**
2. The entry type, date, and author
3. The title and the text of the entry
4. **Site details** -- weather, temperature, workers, company, start and end times, duration, inspector, materials, outcome, and severity, where the entry type uses them. Only fields with a value are shown
5. **Signatures**, when the entry has any
6. **Areas** -- the areas of its photos and the area of the task it came from. Each area opens its page
7. **History** -- collapsed by default. Open it to see when the entry was created and last updated, and by whom

At the top of the page:

- **Edit** opens the entry in the capture screen, where you can change any field
- **Add photos** opens the same capture screen so you can add photos to the entry
- **More actions** (the **⋯** button) holds **Delete**. A confirmation appears before anything is removed, and the photos on the entry are deleted with it

Signed entries cannot be edited, so **Edit** and **Add photos** are hidden on them. **Delete** stays in the **⋯** menu on signed entries. Entries that Cornerstone creates by itself (such as a task status change) have no actions.

## Site Diary and History

The **Site diary** shows the entries you wrote. It does not show automatic events. Cornerstone records those on the **History** page, which you can open from the **History** link at the top of the Site diary.

If you want to see your own entries next to the automatic changes, turn on **Include site diary entries** on the History page. See [History](automatic-events) for the filters and the kinds of changes listed there.
