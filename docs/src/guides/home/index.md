# Home

**Home** is the first page you see after you log in. It answers one question: **what needs me?** Things that are late, waiting for a decision, or ready to act on come first. Below them you find this week's dates, a few headline numbers, and the latest entries from the site.

Home replaces the old project dashboard. If you remember cards such as Budget Summary or Mini Gantt, see [What moved where](/guides/home/what-moved-where) for where each one went.

:::note
The examples on this page use invented names and amounts. Your own numbers will look different.
:::

## Page layout

From top to bottom, Home shows:

1. **Needs attention**: rows that ask for an action.
2. **Tidy up**: small clean-up jobs. This section can be collapsed.
3. **This week**: the critical path line, then the next 7 days, with a "Next 14 days" button.
4. **Pulse**: three tiles with headline numbers.
5. **Latest from the site**: the three most recent diary entries you wrote yourself.
6. A footer link called **All activity**, which opens **History**, the list of everything that happened in the project.

The heading shows the house name and today's date. The **Page actions** button (three dots, `⋯`) at the top right has **Customize…** and **What moved where**.

Sections you have hidden do not appear at all. See [Customize Home](#customize-home).

## Needs attention

Needs attention lists the things that are late, overdue, due soon, or ready for you to act on. The number in the heading, for example "Needs attention (5)", is the number of rows. A group of items counts as one row.

Each row has:

- A **label** that says how urgent it is: **Late**, **Overdue**, **Due**, **Ready**, or **Follow up**.
- The **name** of the thing (a task, invoice, milestone, purchase, and so on). Select the name to open it.
- A **sentence** that says what is wrong, with the date.
- **Action buttons**, such as **Mark done**, **Start**, **Mark paid**, or **Reschedule**. The buttons apply the change straight away. For changes you can take back, a short **Undo** notice appears.
- **Not now**, which hides the row for 7 days.

The first 8 rows show. If there are more, select **Show all (n)**. Select **Show fewer** to collapse the list again.

Here is an example of a late task, with invented names:

> **Late** · *Install the kitchen cabinets* · Kitchen area
> In progress, 3 days late (planned end 14 March 2027).
> [Mark done] [Reschedule] Not now

Needs attention always shows. You cannot hide it, because it is the reason Home exists.

### Not now

**Not now** hides a row for 7 days. The row leaves the list and the count. A notice says "Hidden for 7 days." and has an **Undo** button for a few seconds.

If rows are hidden, a line below the list says "n hidden for now", with a **Show** button. Hidden rows then appear with "Back on" and a date. **Show again** brings one back early.

### Catch-up review

When tasks are late, a line appears above the list, for example "4 tasks are late". Select **Review all** to open the catch-up dialog. It lists every late task, with no 8-row limit. Work through it with the same buttons as on Home. When the last one is done, the dialog says **All caught up.**

Select **Close** when you are finished. The count on Home updates straight away.

## Tidy up

Tidy up holds smaller jobs that are not urgent, such as a finished task that still has no actual end date. The section appears only when there is something to tidy. Select the heading to collapse or expand it. Home remembers your choice.

## This week

**This week** shows what is planned over the next few weeks.

- The **critical path line** at the top names the task that decides the finish date. For example: "Critical path: Lay the hall tiles must end by 2 November 2026 · 12 days left." If the task is already late, the line says it "should have ended" on that date.
- Each day with items has its own group, starting with **Today** and **Tomorrow**.
- Each item shows its kind: **Starts**, **Ends**, **Payment due**, **Delivery**, or **Milestone**.
- An item marked **Held up** names the task it waits for, on a line starting with "Waits for".

Items that already appear in Needs attention are not repeated here.

Select **Next 14 days** to show the two weeks after the first seven days. Select **Open schedule** to go to the full schedule.

## Pulse

Pulse has three tiles. Select a tile to open the matching page.

| Tile | What it shows | Opens |
|------|---------------|-------|
| **Left to spend** | The amount you can still spend, with a range from the worst case to the best case. It shows **Over budget** when the amount is below zero, and **On budget** otherwise. | Money > Overview |
| **Still to pay** | How many invoices are open, for example "2 open invoices". | Invoices, oldest due first |
| **Progress** | How many tasks are done, for example "12 of 40 tasks done", with a bar and the number in progress. | Tasks |

Left to spend needs two things before it can show a figure: at least one funding source with an amount, and at least one cost. Until then the tile shows an empty figure and the line "Add a funding source and a cost to see this." It links to Financing.

## Latest from the site

This section shows the last three diary entries you wrote yourself, photos first. Automatic diary events are left out here. To see everything, select **Site diary**. If there are no entries yet, select **Log today** to write the first one.

## Set up your build

Until the basics are in place, Home shows a checklist called **Set up your build** instead of Needs attention, Tidy up, This week, Pulse, and Latest from the site. Each step has a short note that says why it matters.

| Step | Done when |
|------|-----------|
| Name your house | You have entered a house name. |
| Add your areas | You have at least one area, such as a room, a floor, or an outside space. |
| Add your first funding source | You have a funding source with an amount above zero. Own funds count once they have an amount. |
| Add your first companies | You have at least one company. |
| Plan your first tasks | At least one task has a planned date and a planned cost. |
| Invite your household | Admins only. More than one user exists. |
| Check backups | Admins only. A backup schedule is set, or a backup exists. |
| Log your first day on site | You have at least one saved diary entry. |

Members see six steps, because the two admin steps are not shown to them.

Set-up mode ends when steps 1 to 5 are done, or when you select **Skip setup**. Steps 6 to 8 are only reminders and do not keep Home in set-up mode. You can still add everything from the **New** menu.

## Customize Home

To choose which sections show, select **Page actions** (`⋯`), then **Customize…**. Tick or untick the sections. Your choice saves at once. The order of the sections never changes.

- **Needs attention** always shows. The dialog says so.
- **Tidy up** can be hidden, and it can also be collapsed on the page.
- **This week**, **Pulse**, and **Latest from the site** can be hidden.

Customize only changes your own view. Other people in your household keep their own sections.

## On a phone or tablet

Below a width of 1024 pixels, Home shows a **Where are you?** row under the heading.

- It lists up to five areas you photographed most recently, such as "Ground floor · Hall". Select one to start a new photo entry with that area already set.
- **Other area** starts a photo entry without an area.
- A **Last log** line shows your most recent diary entry, with its date, title, and photo count. Select it to open the entry.

The row only looks at your most recent diary entries. An area you photographed only on older entries is not offered here. Pick it from the photo screen instead.

## Notices and help

The first time you open the new Home, a short notice says **Home has changed.** It links to [What moved where](/guides/home/what-moved-where). Select **Dismiss** to close it. It does not come back.

You can open the same help page at any time from **Page actions** (`⋯`) and then **What moved where**. It opens in a new tab.
