# What moved where

The project dashboard with its cards is gone. **Home** replaces it, and the things the cards showed now live in a few clear sections. This page shows where each old card went and what the labels on Home mean.

Use this page as a reference. You can open it any time from **Page actions** (`⋯`) and then **What moved where**.

## Where each card went

| Old card | Where it is now | Notes |
|----------|-----------------|-------|
| **Budget Summary** | **Pulse**, tile **Left to spend** | Select the tile to open Money > Overview. |
| **Task Progress** | **Pulse**, tile **Progress** | Select the tile to open the task list. |
| **Invoice Pipeline** | **Money > Invoices**, the **To pay** chip and its **Still to pay** footer | The chip lists every open invoice and progress payment. Invoices that need action also appear as rows in **Needs attention**, and the **Still to pay** tile in **Pulse** opens the **To pay** chip. |
| **Upcoming Milestones** | **Needs attention** (late milestones) and **This week** (milestones on their target date) | |
| **Critical Path** | **This week**, the first line | The line names the task that decides the finish date. |
| **Mini Gantt** | **This week**, plus **Open schedule** under the list | For the full picture, open the schedule. |
| **Recent Diary** | **Latest from the site** | Shows the last three diary entries you wrote yourself. Select **Site diary** for the rest. |
| **Source Utilization** | Money, budget funding rows, and **Financing** | Each funding source shows how much of it is used. |
| **Subsidy Pipeline** | Money > Financing > Grants, and deadlines in **Needs attention** | Grants with a deadline show up as rows when action is needed. |
| **Quick Actions** | The **New** menu at the top of the page | Creating a task, an invoice, a diary entry, or any other item starts there. |

Some old cards are split across two places on purpose. A payment that needs you appears as a row in **Needs attention**, and the total you still owe appears in **Pulse**.

The old invoice summary cards are gone too. Their counts now sit on the status chips of **Money > Invoices** (for example **To pay (12)**), and the total you still owe sits in the **Still to pay** footer under the list. The Offers summary appears when you select the **Offers** chip. Filtered totals show **Still to pay in this list**, and the **Ready to submit** amount is not shown on any page yet.

## Who

**Who** on a task is one picker with two groups: companies and household members. Type to search, and pick either a company or a household member. **Household member** is a person with their own sign-in to this project, and a task can be given to them. Picking one replaces the other, so a task never has both.

## Companies

Each company has its own page under **Companies** in the main navigation. Open it to see the company's **Still to pay** and **Paid** totals, its contact persons with a **Call** button, and its invoices, offers, progress payments, tasks, site diary entries and history. To pay an invoice, use **Money > Invoices** as before. See [Invoices & Companies](/guides/budget/vendors-and-invoices#company-page) for the full page.

## What the labels mean

The rows in **Needs attention** and **Tidy up** each carry a label. The label tells you how urgent the row is. The words on each row describe what happened, with a date.

| Label | Meaning | Example sentence |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| **Late** | A task or milestone is past its planned date and still not finished. | In progress, 3 days late (planned end 14 March 2027). |
| **Overdue** | A payment is past its due date. | Payment was due 2 March 2027 (5 days ago). |
| **Due** | A payment or item is due soon. | Payment due 30 March 2027 (in 6 days). |
| **Ready** | Items are complete and ready to send, for example to the bank. | 2 items ready to submit. |
| **Follow up** | Something is waiting for a decision or a reply, such as an offer or a grant deadline. | Offer due 9 April 2027 (in 18 days). |
| **Tidy up** | A small clean-up job that is not urgent. | Shown under its own heading, not under Needs attention. |

## Renamed budget words

Some words in the budget pages changed. The screens do the same job as before.

| Earlier word | Now | Where you see it |
|--------------|-----|------------------|
| **Budget line** | **Cost line** | The **Cost lines** section on a task, purchase, offer or invoice. **Add cost line** adds one; **Add lines (grid)** enters several at once. |
| **Confidence** | **Basis** | The setting on each cost line: **Own estimate**, **Professional estimate**, **Offer** or **Invoice**. |
| **Itemize** | **Split** | The **Not yet split** row on an offer or invoice. The **Itemize** action on a linked document still opens **Split with AI**. |
| **Remaining budget** | **Left to spend** | The headline on Money > Overview, and the **Left to spend** tile on Home. |
| **Available funds** | **Funding total** | The **Funding total** fact on Money > Overview. Each funding source is listed under **Funding sources**, with its **Allocated** bar. |
| **Cost basis** | **Payment** | The **Payment** setting on Money > Overview: **All**, **Paid** or **Not yet paid**. |
| **Outstanding** | **Not yet paid** | The **Not yet paid** payment setting on Money > Overview. The **Still to pay** fact keeps its name and counts your open invoices. |
| **Min**, **Avg** and **Max** | **Low**, **Expected** and **High** | The **Show estimates at** setting on Money > Overview. |
| **Add** menu on the budget page | Removed | Create items from the **New** menu at the top of the page. |

Two more words come up often:

- **Held up**: the task cannot start or end on time because another task it depends on is late. The line underneath says "Waits for" and names that task.
- **Critical path**: the chain of tasks that decides the finish date. A delay on it moves the finish date. A delay elsewhere may not.

All names, amounts, and dates on this page are invented examples.
